When 2+ pieces share a cell: spread apart, or stack on top of each other?

Right now (Option A), pieces sharing a cell nudge into a 2×2 quadrant pattern so up to 4 stay visible side by side. You asked whether they could instead sit on top of each other — here's what that looks like, at 2, 3, and 4 pieces on one cell.

Opinion

RecommendedKeep the current side-by-side spread (Option A) as the default, but it's a reasonable question — full overlap has one real advantage worth knowing about: at small board-cell sizes on a phone, 4 pieces spread into quadrants can each shrink to a sliver that's hard to tap accurately. Stacking keeps every piece full-size and tappable, at the cost of hiding how many are actually there unless you add a count badge or let players tap through the stack.

Option C (cascade) is the best middle ground if you want to change it: pieces stay full-size and it's visually obvious more than one is there, without needing a separate badge or tap-to-cycle interaction.
