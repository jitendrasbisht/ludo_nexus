End-of-game flow · POC, not implemented

Stay on the finished board instead of auto-popping a dialog

Right now, the instant the match ends, a modal "Game over!" dialog covers the board automatically. You want the board itself to stay visible (with everyone's rank cards showing) -- the player chooses when to see results or leave, instead of it being forced on them.

Proposed change

When the match ends, no dialog pops up automatically. The bottom status pill (which currently shows things like "Red's turn continues...") instead becomes a tappable action button reading "Game over -- tap to see final standings". The board stays fully visible behind it, showing every finished player's rank card. Tapping the pill opens the same standings dialog as before (Play Again / Back to Setup).
