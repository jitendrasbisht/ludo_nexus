Each loops continuously here so you can watch the motion (in the real app it plays once, then auto-navigates to the setup screen after ~1.5-2s). All reuse the app's own navy background and dice-token art style.

Opinion

RecommendedOption A -- bounce & glow. It's the most "alive" of the three without being distracting, and the soft glow pulse behind the dice echoes the glossy-token look already established for the pieces and app icon. Option C is the safer, faster fallback if you'd rather players get to the setup screen as quickly as possible. Of the new batch, Option D -- board reveal is the strongest alternative: it previews the actual game board instead of just showing the icon again, which is a nice bit of storytelling for a first launch.
