Play continues after 1st place, until only 1 player is left

Currently the whole game ends the instant any player finishes all 4 pieces. This POC shows the new flow: a quick banner when someone finishes (no popup, no pause), play continues automatically with the rest, and a final rankings dialog only appears once just one player remains -- they're automatically last place, since there's no one left to play against them.
