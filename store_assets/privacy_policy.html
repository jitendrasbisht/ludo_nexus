Privacy Policy

Effective August 14, 2026

The short version: Ludo Nexus doesn't collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. The app works entirely offline, on your device, with no account, no analytics, and no ads.

What we collect

Nothing — by design

Ludo Nexus is a local, pass-and-play board game. There's no login, no user accounts, no network connection required to play, and no data of any kind is sent anywhere. Everything that happens in a game — player names, whose turn it is, piece positions — exists only in the app's memory on your device while you're playing, and is discarded when the game ends.

Player photos

If you choose to set a photo for a player (instead of the default colored token), the app saves that image inside its own private storage on your device, using it only to display that player's piece during the game. That photo is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone — including us. It stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Permissions the app asks for

Ludo Nexus may ask for camera or photo library access solely so you can pick a player's photo, as described above. It does not request access to contacts, location, microphone, or anything else, and doesn't use any permission it's granted for tracking or advertising purposes.

Children's privacy

Ludo Nexus is a general-audience game suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, including children, there is nothing to disclose or protect beyond what's already described in this policy.

If this ever changes

If a future version of Ludo Nexus adds anything that changes this — for example, optional ads to support the app — this policy will be updated before that change ships, and the update will clearly describe what's new and why. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy; if you'd rather not, you're free to stop using the app or uninstall it at any time.
