## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Ludo Nexus brings the classic dice-and-board game everyone grew up with straight to your phone — no internet required.

**Play your way**
- Pass-and-play with 2, 3, or 4 players on one device
- Fill empty seats with AI bots at your choice of difficulty
- No sign-up, no login, no ads interrupting your game — just Ludo

**Smart, fair rules**
- Capture an opponent's piece or get one all the way home and you earn another roll
- Rolling three 6s (or three bonus rolls) in a row forfeits the turn, so no one can run away with the board
- Play continues after the first player finishes — everyone races for 1st through last place, not just the win

**Built for the game, not just the app**
- A full board that actually looks like Ludo — proper tracks, safe cells, and home stretches
- Glossy, colorful pieces that are easy to tell apart and satisfying to move
- See exactly whose turn it is and what's happening at a glance

**Completely offline**
Ludo Nexus doesn't need a network connection to play. No accounts, no data collection, no tracking — just install and play, on a plane, on the road, or anywhere else.

Whether it's a quick match against the bots or a full house passing the phone around, Ludo Nexus is Ludo the way you remember it.
