## From Abhiroop (2026-08-15/16)

1. **Color choice in 2-player + bot games**: When starting a game with 2 players where one is a bot, the human player should be able to choose which color they play as. Currently color assignment isn't user-selectable in this setup.
2. **Faster animations**: Dice roll animation and piece movement animation both feel slow — speed both up.
3. **Highlight eligible pieces after roll**: After a player rolls the dice, all of *that player's* pieces that are legal to move with the rolled number should be visually highlighted, so the player can see their options at a glance before tapping one.
4. **3D dice art**: Current dice graphic looks flat/raw — wants a 3D-styled die (better shading/depth), not just a 2D icon.
