← How to Play
Basics
🎯

Goal

Get all 4 of your pieces from your base, around the board, and into your home column before the others.

🎲

Starting a piece

Pieces stay in base until you roll a 6 — that lets one piece step onto the board.

Bonus Rolls
✨

Roll again

Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent, or getting a piece home all earn you another roll immediately.

⛔

Three in a row

Chain three bonus rolls in a row (any mix of 6s, captures, finishes) and your turn ends immediately — no move on that third roll.

Captures & Safety
💥

Capturing

Land exactly on an opponent's piece (on a non-safe cell) and it's sent all the way back to their base.

✦

Safe cells

Star-marked cells protect any piece sitting there — no captures can happen on them.

Winning
🏆

Play continues

The match doesn't stop when someone finishes first — everyone keeps playing to settle 2nd, 3rd, and last place too.
